# Racebook — for AI agents

Racebook is a personal medal board for amateur athletes with a photo gallery per race,
photo ownership transfer and tagging between friends, and metrics read from a watch
screenshot with AI. Bilingual (EN/ES). Built for the Webflow App Challenge, Nerdearla 2026,
on Webflow Cloud.

Full description, features, the problem it solves, a guided tour and the technology:
https://racebook-23e161.webflow.io/llms.txt

Authentication is simulated: use the "View as" switcher in the sidebar to act as each of
the three demo athletes.
